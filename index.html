Privacy & security
Data controls

Privacy & retention

Owner responsibility

Business AI stores customer contact details, enquiry details, lead activity, bookings/actions and limited audit records so you can manage enquiries. Set a review period; you remain responsible for deciding when lawful deletion/anonymisation is appropriate. This is a technical foundation, not legal advice or automatic compliance.

Owner-only customer-data export and anonymisation are handled on request during the Pilot. To export or anonymise an individual customer record, contact your Pilot support contact.

Support

Pilot help

Owner guide
Start here: finish your business profile, copy the customer enquiry link, then send a test enquiry. New enquiries become leads; use Actions for work your team needs to do next. A handover means the AI has flagged something for human attention — it does not mean anyone has contacted the customer. Bookings are requests until you confirm them. Team invitations are shared by copying a secure link; Business AI does not send the invitation email during this Pilot. Review your retention policy and export/anonymise individual customer records deliberately. Voice calls remain Coming Soon. Marketing publishing is available only after the owner connects an eligible account and explicitly approves a draft; billing availability is shown in the Billing section. If something behaves unexpectedly, stop using that workflow, record the time and action, then report it to your Pilot contact.
