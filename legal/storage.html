Storage & Cookie Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective 27 September 2026

Pilot identity notice. Business AI is currently an invitation-only UK Pilot. The service provider's final legal/trading name, geographic address and direct contact email are not yet published. Those details must be completed before any paid public launch. Pilot users should use the support route supplied with their invitation.

1. What this notice covers

This notice explains cookies and similar storage/access technologies used by the Business AI Pilot. These technologies can include browser storage and service-worker caches as well as traditional cookies.

2. Essential authentication storage

The authenticated owner app uses Supabase Auth. Its browser client may use browser storage to maintain your signed-in session. This is used to provide the login/session service you request and is treated as essential to authenticated app operation.

3. Temporary conversation storage

The owner-side AI test tool uses sessionStorage to keep the current tenant-scoped test conversation available during the browser session. It is cleared when the relevant private state is cleared and is not used for advertising.

4. Static app cache

The Business AI service worker may cache a small set of static assets such as the app manifest and icons so the installed app shell works reliably. It does not intentionally cache authenticated API responses, access tokens, customer records or page navigations.

5. Analytics and advertising

The current Pilot does not intentionally configure advertising cookies or behavioural analytics trackers. If non-essential tracking is introduced later, this notice and the consent controls will be updated before that tracking is enabled where consent is required.

6. Controls

You can clear site data through your browser settings, but removing essential authentication storage may sign you out or prevent some requested app functions from working until the storage is recreated.
