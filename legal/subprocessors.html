Sub-processor Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective 27 September 2026

Pilot identity notice. Business AI is currently an invitation-only UK Pilot. The service provider's final legal/trading name, geographic address and direct contact email are not yet published. Those details must be completed before any paid public launch. Pilot users should use the support route supplied with their invitation.

These providers may process personal data when needed to provide the Business AI Pilot. The exact data sent depends on which features a business enables.

Changes

Business AI may add or replace providers as the service changes. Material changes affecting Controller Personal Data will be reflected here and, where appropriate, notified to business owners so they can raise a reasonable data-protection objection.
