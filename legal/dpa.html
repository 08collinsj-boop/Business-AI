This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the Business AI Pilot Terms where a business uses Business AI to process personal data on its behalf. The business account holder is the Controller and the Business AI Pilot operator is the Processor for the processing described below.

1. Processing details

Subject matter: customer enquiry handling and related lead, action, booking, business-knowledge and support workflows. Duration: for the period the Controller uses the service plus any limited retention needed for secure return/deletion and legal obligations. Purpose: to provide the Business AI service on the Controller's documented instructions.

Data subjects

Customers and prospective customers of the Controller; authorised business users; and other people whose information the Controller lawfully submits to the service.

Types of personal data

Names, contact details, enquiry content, service/booking requirements, lead notes, communications, and other personal information the Controller chooses to submit. Businesses must not deliberately use Business AI to collect unnecessary special-category data, criminal-offence data or payment-card details.

2. Controller instructions

Business AI will process Controller Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including instructions provided through configured product settings and authorised user actions, unless UK law requires otherwise. If a legal requirement prevents following an instruction, Business AI will inform the Controller where legally permitted.

3. Confidentiality

Business AI will ensure that people authorised to process Controller Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

4. Security

Business AI will maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the Pilot's risks, including authenticated tenant boundaries, role-based permissions, row-level security, server-side secret handling, access controls, audit records and secure provider connections. The Controller is responsible for its own user access, device security and lawful configuration.

5. Sub-processors

The Controller gives general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in the Sub-processor Notice. Business AI will maintain that list and should provide reasonable notice of material additions or replacements. The Controller may raise a reasonable data-protection objection through its Pilot support route. Business AI remains responsible for imposing appropriate data-protection obligations on its sub-processors.

6. Individual rights

Taking account of the nature of processing, Business AI will provide reasonable technical assistance to help the Controller respond to requests to exercise data-protection rights. The Pilot includes tenant-scoped export/anonymisation foundations for individual customer records.

7. Compliance assistance and incidents

Taking account of the processing and information available, Business AI will provide reasonable assistance with security obligations, personal-data-breach handling, data-protection impact assessments and regulator consultation where those duties apply to the Controller. Business AI will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Controller Personal Data.

8. International transfers

Business AI will not intentionally make a restricted transfer of Controller Personal Data except where a lawful UK transfer mechanism is available and any required transfer assessment is completed. The Controller authorises transfers inherent in the listed sub-processors subject to those safeguards.

9. Return and deletion

At the end of the processor services, and at the Controller's choice, Business AI will delete or return Controller Personal Data and delete remaining copies, unless UK law requires storage. Secure deletion may take a reasonable period to complete across backups and provider systems.

10. Information and audit

Business AI will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 obligations and will permit reasonable audits or inspections, subject to appropriate confidentiality, security, proportionality and advance-notice arrangements. Existing independent reports or provider documentation may be used where appropriate.

11. Controller obligations

The Controller is responsible for the lawfulness, fairness and transparency of its processing; the accuracy and necessity of submitted data; responding to individuals; selecting appropriate retention; and ensuring its instructions comply with applicable law.

12. Priority and changes

If this DPA conflicts with the general Terms on the processing of Controller Personal Data, this DPA takes priority. Material DPA changes will be versioned and may require renewed owner acceptance.
