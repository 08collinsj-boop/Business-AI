Data deletion instructions

Last updated 5 October 2026

Service provider. Business AI is operated by James David Lewis Collins, trading as Business AI (sole trader). Contact: support@getbusiness-ai.com. Current service-provider details also appear in our Privacy Notice.

You can request deletion of personal information held by Business AI, including information associated with the Business-AI Pages Facebook / Meta integration. You do not need to log in, reconnect Facebook or provide a password to make a request. These instructions supplement our Privacy Notice and do not replace or limit your UK GDPR rights.

1. What you can ask us to delete

Tell us whether you want to delete particular personal information, close your own Business AI account, remove a Facebook / Meta connection, or close an entire business workspace if you are authorised to act for that business. Depending on the features used, your request may cover:

  • Account details, authentication records, business-user profile information and memberships.
  • Business profile and configuration information, Business Knowledge files and extracted facts, marketing drafts and images, schedules and publication records.
  • Customer names, contact details and enquiry messages, and related leads, actions, bookings and enquiry-tracking records.
  • Facebook / Meta user and Page identifiers, connected-account names, connection records, permissions recorded for the connection and stored access tokens.
  • Support, feedback, billing and security records, subject to any applicable lawful retention explained below.

Closing your own user account does not automatically authorise deletion of a shared business workspace or another person's information. We will confirm the scope and your authority before acting on business-wide requests.

2. How to make a request

Email support@getbusiness-ai.com. A subject such as “Business AI data deletion request” is helpful but is not required. Requests do not need a special form or legal wording, and valid requests made through our other published contact routes will also be considered.

Please include the information you know that will help us locate the relevant records:

  • Your name and the email address used for Business AI, or the contact details used in your enquiry.
  • The business or workspace name and, if available, its public Business AI link or business identifier.
  • What you want deleted: your account, an entire authorised business workspace, specific records, or only Facebook / Meta connection data.
  • For Facebook / Meta requests, the connected Page name or Page URL and any user or Page identifier you already know. You do not need to obtain an access token.
  • For an enquiry, an approximate date and any enquiry or booking reference you have.
  • A reply address, and whether you are acting for yourself, as an authorised business representative or on another person's behalf.

Missing a reference or identifier does not prevent you from making a request. We may ask for further information only where reasonably necessary to locate the data or verify identity or authority. Do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, payment-card details or identity documents unless we have specifically explained why proportionate identity evidence is needed and provided a suitable way to supply it.

3. Customer enquiries sent to another business

If you contacted a business through its Business AI enquiry page, that business is normally the controller of your enquiry information. Ask that business to delete the relevant information first where possible. Business AI acts as its processor and assists on the business's documented instructions under our Data Processing Agreement. You can also contact us at the email above if you need help identifying the correct route; this does not restrict your ability to exercise your rights.

Business owners can request deletion or return of their customer data when processor services end, as set out in the DPA. We will distinguish that request from deletion of Business AI's own account, billing or security records.

4. Facebook / Meta connection data and tokens

For a verified request to remove a connection, we will remove the relevant Facebook / Meta connection data and stored user and Page access tokens from Business AI's active systems, and stop using that connection for further access or publishing. Tokens are stored encrypted. Where related marketing or publication records also contain personal information within your request, we will assess those records separately for deletion or anonymisation and explain any lawful retention.

The Pilot has an authenticated disconnect function which deletes the stored connection and its linked social-account records. A full account, workspace or privacy-rights request is handled through the contact route above; this page is an instructions page, not an automated Meta deletion callback.

You can also remove Business-AI Pages from your Facebook / Meta connected-app or business-integration settings to withdraw its access. Removing access in Meta is separate from asking Business AI to delete data already held here: please contact us for that deletion. You do not need to reconnect the app to make the request.

Disconnecting or deleting Business AI data does not delete your Facebook account or Page, or automatically remove posts already published or scheduled directly on Facebook. Review those posts and schedules in Meta's own tools if you want to remove or cancel them. Meta handles information it holds under its own privacy policies.

5. Retention, backups and the outcome

The right to erasure applies in the circumstances set out in UK data-protection law. We will delete personal information where required, or irreversibly anonymise it where appropriate. Some information must remain where a legal obligation requires it, such as applicable accounting or tax records. Limited information may also be retained where another lawful exception applies, such as the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. We will explain what is retained, why, and the applicable retention or review basis in our response.

We do not retain data indefinitely merely because an account has existed. The Privacy Notice's lead-data and audit-record review settings are review periods, not promises of automatic deletion or a reason to refuse a valid erasure request. Account, billing, security, legal and support records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their purpose or a legal requirement.

Secure deletion across backups and provider systems may take additional time. We will explain any relevant limits and how remaining copies are handled, rather than promising immediate deletion from every backup. Where required, we will notify recipients of erasure and assist with deletion by providers.

6. Response and further rights

We will respond without undue delay and normally within one month, in accordance with UK GDPR. If necessary identity information is reasonably requested, we will explain how that affects the response deadline. Where the law permits an extension for a complex request or multiple requests, we will tell you within the initial month and explain the reason. Making a request is normally free.

Our response will confirm the action taken or explain any refusal or partial retention. You can ask us to reconsider, complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or seek a judicial remedy. You also retain applicable rights of access, correction, restriction, objection and portability described in our Privacy Notice.
