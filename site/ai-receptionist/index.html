Common questions

What small businesses usually want to know.

Does Business AI replace my staff?

No. It is designed to handle routine first contact and admin while your team keeps control and can take over when needed.

Can I control how much the AI handles?

Yes. Business AI includes Human-first, Balanced and AI-first handling modes so the owner can choose the level of automation.

What happens after an enquiry?

Depending on the conversation and your setup, the details can become a lead, action or booking request for review.

Is this only for large companies?

No. Business AI is designed around the needs of small and owner-led businesses.
