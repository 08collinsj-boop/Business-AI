Colzo / Pilot preparation

A small pilot, by approval.

We are preparing a limited Colzo pilot for small businesses. Accounts are granted only after approval and final security checks.

The free pilot lasts exactly 14 days from activation, supports up to two authorised users and requires no payment card. There is no automatic paid subscription or conversion.

You can request a demonstration without sharing customer data. Expressing interest does not create an account or start the 14 days.

The team will discuss suitability, verify business ownership and identity, and agree setup before activating access. The current pilot covers web enquiries; telephone answering and AI Marketing are not included in this scoped pilot.
