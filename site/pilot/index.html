Protected review · No external access

Help shape a better working day.

External pilot access is not currently available. We are exploring whether Colzo can help businesses manage website enquiries, organise booking requests and follow up with customers.

What evaluation would cover

Routine website enquiries using approved business information, capturing details, human-reviewed requests and booking requests (not confirmed appointments). Telephone answering and AI Marketing are outside this scoped pilot. No live external integrations are offered in this demonstration.

Approved access would last exactly 14 days from activation for up to two authorised users, with no card or automatic subscription conversion. Approval depends on product fit and final security checks.
