Common questions

What small businesses usually want to know.

What is customer enquiry management?

It is the process of receiving customer questions or requests, recording the useful details and making sure there is a clear next action.

How is this different from a normal inbox?

Business AI connects the enquiry to leads, actions and booking workflows rather than leaving every message as an isolated conversation.

Can I still handle enquiries myself?

Yes. Business AI is designed around owner control and supports a Human-first mode as well as more automated options.

Does it include a customer portal?

Business AI includes a customer experience that can support business discovery, enquiries and account-based tracking where enabled.
