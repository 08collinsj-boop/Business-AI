01Sign in and get startedBusiness account · Dashboard+
  1. Open app.getbusiness-ai.com.
  2. Select Business.
  3. Enter your business account email and password.
  4. After sign-in, the Dashboard gives you the quickest view of enquiries, actions and bookings that need attention.
02Understand your DashboardHome · Daily overview+
  1. Open Home.
  2. Use the top cards to check new leads, items needing attention, actions due and total enquiries.
  3. Open an item from the dashboard to move directly into the relevant workflow.
  4. Use the pipeline view to see where active opportunities are sitting.
03Manage a leadLeads · Follow-up+
  1. Open Leads from the navigation.
  2. Choose a lead to view the enquiry and customer details.
  3. Review the current status and any suggested next step.
  4. Update the lead as you follow up so your pipeline stays accurate.
04Create and complete an actionActions · Tasks+
  1. Open Actions.
  2. Use New action when you want to add a follow-up or task yourself.
  3. Add the task details and due information.
  4. Mark the action complete when it has been dealt with.
05Manage a bookingBookings · Appointments+
  1. Open Bookings.
  2. Review new booking requests and upcoming work.
  3. Open a booking to see the customer and appointment details.
  4. When the work is finished, complete the booking so the follow-up flow can continue.
06Set up the AI ReceptionistAI Receptionist · Control+
  1. Open AI Receptionist.
  2. Check the business information and knowledge the AI can use.
  3. Choose the handling style that fits your business: Human-first, Balanced or AI-first.
  4. Use the test chat to see how the receptionist responds before customers use it.
07Create a Marketing postMarketing · Drafts+
  1. Open Marketing and start a new generation.
  2. Choose the content type, platform and tone.
  3. Review the generated copy and image before approving it.
  4. Save it as a draft, add it to your content plan, or schedule/publish when your connected channel is ready.
08Add Business KnowledgeKnowledge · Files+
  1. Open the Business Knowledge area.
  2. Add useful business information such as services, FAQs, menus or price information.
  3. Upload supported files where available.
  4. Keep the information current so the AI has better context when answering customers.
09Use the Customer PortalCustomers · Guest access+
  1. From the sign-in screen, switch to Customer.
  2. Customers can sign in to keep track of activity or choose Continue as guest.
  3. Guest users can search the directory and open a business.
  4. From there they can start an enquiry with that business.
10Manage your subscriptionBilling · Plan+
  1. Open Billing from the owner app.
  2. Review your current plan, allowance and any active add-ons.
  3. Use the billing controls to open the secure subscription-management flow.
  4. Any plan changes remain tied to the business account you are signed into.
