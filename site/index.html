The thinking behind Colzo

Your business, with more breathing room.

Technology should work around how people actually run their businesses. Colzo is designed to reduce the constant switching between messages, bookings, records and follow-ups.

What does Colzo bring together?

Enquiries, bookings, customers, follow-ups and assisted business communications, depending on your plan and enabled features.

Does AI send messages without approval?

The pilot design prioritises human oversight and approval controls. Live automation availability must be verified before release.

What's included in the pilot?

The proposed pilot runs for 14 days without card details. Activation depends on completion of the outstanding security and operational acceptance.

How can I register pilot interest?

The pilot-interest page is open, but access requires approval after security and operational checks. Registration does not create an account and does not convert automatically to a paid subscription.
