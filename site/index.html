Frequently asked questions

What small businesses usually want to know.

Business AI is designed to assist with routine work while leaving important business decisions with you.

Does Business AI answer phone calls?

The current AI Receptionist is focused on web enquiries. Voice features are separate from the core web-enquiry workflow.

Can I control when a person takes over?

Yes. Business AI supports human-first, balanced and AI-first handling so you can decide how much routine work the AI handles.

What information does the AI use?

You can add business information such as services, FAQs and pricing so routine answers have useful context.

Is Marketing included?

AI Marketing is available as an optional add-on for business-aware content, drafts and content planning.
