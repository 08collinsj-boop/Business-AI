# Pilot operations and release gates

Draft operating procedure, 25 October 2026. Real onboarding remains blocked.

## Eligibility and approval

Prioritise 3–5 local businesses with an owner who can review website enquiries and provide feedback. Start with James's two personal contacts; do not contact them on his behalf. Confirm actual website enquiry use, lawful business information, two named users, a responsible owner and a suitable low-risk workflow. Exclude sensitive/high-risk customer workflows pending separate review.

Keep a minimal prospect record: business, approved contact channel, source/referral, last contact date, permission/response status and one next action. States: referred → permission to demo → demo agreed → interested → release-approved → invited → active → expired/withdrawn. Keep unresponsive and declined separate. Record opt-outs and do not pursue them. Do not label an unresponsive contact interested.

Approve each exact business and user mapping before provisioning. Record approver, approval time/reference, scope, verified owner attestation, two-seat allocation, activation and exact 336-hour expiry. The existing private staged operations are review proposals, not a live operator service. Do not bypass account or membership guards.

## Mandatory gates before invitations

1. Actual Development records, Auth state, Storage bytes, roles/ACLs, extensions and essential configuration restored privately and verified in an isolated compatible runtime. Independent recovery sign-off required. Synthetic tests are insufficient.
2. Migration reconciliation tested on old and fresh paths; no name-only history repair. Review both same-version SQL divergences and preserve Development-only WhatsApp isolation.
3. Secure operator workflow integrated and reviewed in isolation; current staged SQL stays outside automatic migrations. Scoped grant, expiry, revocation, invitation, seat-race and audit rollback acceptance pass.
4. Exact final-SHA Preview approved; two authorised Development identities complete actual email verification, login/logout/refresh, MFA, password recovery and tenant-isolation acceptance. No older-Pilot factor substitution.
5. Owner/customer journeys, integration failure recovery, no-booking refusal, approved-fact answers and persistence checked on the final Preview. Physical iPhone Safari/PWA acceptance complete.
6. Confirm operator legal identity, approved public business address/contact, actual processors/AI routing, pilot participation terms, support process and an exact retention/return/deletion schedule. Obtain appropriate professional review. iubenda verification alone is not compliance acceptance.
7. James approves the exact accounts, activation times and invitation content. No automatic email or DM sender is enabled by this procedure.

## Controlled activation

Use a private operator identity and approved secure token generation/delivery. No public self-grant. Verify project mapping to Development and approval evidence before every operation. Record grant/audit identifiers without raw tokens. Check email/MFA and ownership; use exactly two separately named seats. Test negative direct API/RLS access and paid-feature denials. Setup with fictional data and activate only after the approved integration checklist. Confirm that existing paid/legacy access remains unaffected.

## Support and monitoring

Named pilot coordinator: to be assigned by James before activation. Support route: `support@getbusiness-ai.com`; support hours, escalation contacts and response expectations must be agreed. Review operational errors, usage/allowance, failed persistence, handovers and unusual access within the free authorised tooling. No paid AI calls or customer-data exports for diagnostics without approval.

For a security incident or cross-business disclosure: contain the affected approved workflow through the authorised process, preserve minimal private evidence, record discovery/impact/actions, assess notification duties with the responsible operator and obtain professional advice where needed. Do not delete audit evidence or promise a legal outcome. Never include customer records or secrets in GitHub issues.

For unexpected AI output: capture a minimal redacted example, confirm approved facts and routing, reproduce with synthetic inputs, test the correction and obtain release approval before activation. For data rights/access complaints, verify identity and controller responsibilities through a private process; do not disclose records based solely on an email claim.

## Feedback and end-of-pilot

Propose check-ins on days 3, 7 and 12. Ask what changed in the business's workflow, which tasks were useful and what failed. Measure confirmed demo requests, accepted pilots, completed tasks and concrete feedback; views alone are not sales evidence.

Before expiry, manually prepare the agreed notification through the approved support channel. Access denial must be based on current database state and not depend on a reminder scheduler. Keep the durable expired/revoked designation so it cannot fall back to legacy access. Test the exact boundary and timezone display. Never extend free access by replaying an old request.

Follow the agreed retention/return/deletion schedule; record authorisation and completion without unnecessary personal data. No automatic charge, subscription conversion or new invitation. Future paid interest is optional and separate.
