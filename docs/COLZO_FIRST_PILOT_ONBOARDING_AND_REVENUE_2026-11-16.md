# COLZO — first pilot onboarding, discovery and commercial conversion pack
**Internal draft, 16 November 2026 — NOT a contract, published legal policy, current pricing approval, customer agreement or permission to invite/charge anyone. External pilot: NO-GO.**

## Commercial positioning
**Core:** A simple desktop workspace that makes it easier to see enquiries, bookings, customers and actions in one place; over time, a permissions-led connected operating layer for the software businesses already use. On mobile, stay on top of the business while away from the desk. Avoid selling an imaginary “autonomous AI employee”, live ClickUp/LeadConnector sync or a finished data connector.

**Trial offer proposed by owner:** 14-day free pilot, no card, no subscription commitment; Starter and AI Marketing access during an active pilot only **if** the real feature/access grants are approved and technically verified. No charges or Production activation. Historic proposed plans: Starter £34.99/month, Pro £79.99/month, Business £159.99/month, Marketing add-on £19.99/month — **reconfirm actual current pricing and inclusions before quoting or publishing; these are not an activated billing offer**.

## Prospect and problem qualification — five questions, 10–15 minutes
1. Which admin task takes up the most time each week? Can the owner show an example using entirely fictional data?
2. What tools does the team already use? Which tool is the trusted source for enquiries, contacts, appointments, tasks and billing?
3. Who would actually use Colzo, what are their roles, and who owns permission for provider connections?
4. What would a useful outcome look like after 14 days: minutes saved, fewer missed enquiries, one less manual handoff, a verified appointment workflow?
5. Who can approve a trial, its data-processing terms and an eventual paid budget? A contact who says “interesting” is not yet a buyer.

Potential test audience: North-East trades/service businesses and a **separately consenting** multi-tool organisation. Autism Matters has been mentioned as a potential organisation, **not an authorised customer**; do not access its client, charity-user, staff or provider records without organisation-level approval. Use fictional data until permitted and compliant.

## Participant onboarding checklist — PRIVATE, not to be used until release GO
- Organisation/controller identity and authorised decision maker; appropriate pilot/DPA/Privacy versions accepted
- Exact pilot start/expiry times and named account roles; MFA separately enrolled by each intended person
- Supported scope (native features vs mock-only integration), permitted data classes, explicit ban on sensitive real data if not covered
- Which external application is system of record and who authorises OAuth; avoid credentials in chat/email
- Support alias, realistic response expectation, incident/reporting path, backup owner and process
- Owner's approved data-return, retention, deletion and backup/restore procedure
- Pilot success measure, baseline, review date, early-stop rule and contact method
- Written final GO and separate approval before charging or turning on Production

## 14-day lightweight success scorecard (measured, not guaranteed)
- **Primary outcome:** one chosen manual workflow. Record baseline average minutes per instance, weekly volume, follow-up completion and post-trial actuals.
- **Adoption:** authorised team participants who completed the workflow / eligible participants.
- **Reliability:** count of confirmed successful actions, permission errors, duplicates, and customer-support incidents.
- **Privacy:** number of unauthorised exposures (target **zero**); any such event stops the pilot pending review.
- **Commercial:** decision maker agrees the outcome is useful and confirms willingness/budget to subscribe. Do not use “likes”, shares or demo views as conversion.

Example target: “An owner currently spends 10 minutes per appointment follow-up; can Colzo reduce it to 5 without losing data or making duplicate bookings?” This is a hypothetical target, **not measured Colzo ROI**.
