# Your Colzo pilot

Draft for review. This guide is for approved businesses after release acceptance. It is not an invitation or an activated account.

## Before you start

The pilot is free for exactly 14 days (336 elapsed hours) from activation, with no payment card and no automatic paid conversion. Places are limited and subject to final approval. Your activation and expiry times must be confirmed in writing before access begins. Setup and demonstration can use fictional information before activation.

Colzo currently helps with website enquiries and related administration. It does not answer telephone calls. A booking request is not a confirmed appointment. Customer-visible replies from the new reply feature are outside this free pilot. Use your normal approved contact channel to reply to customers.

## Getting set up

1. Agree the purpose of your pilot: for example, collecting quotation requests from your website. Avoid sensitive information or high-risk workflows.
2. Confirm the business owner, the approved business and up to two named users. Each person uses a separate account. Never share passwords or authenticator codes.
3. Receive account instructions through the approved secure process. Verify your email and set up the required authenticator for this specific Colzo account.
4. Check your business name, services, service area, contact details and opening hours. Only add prices or availability you can stand behind.
5. Review Business Knowledge facts for accuracy before approving them. Remove outdated or unsupported claims. Upload only information you have permission to use.
6. Agree the receptionist handling mode and when it should pass an enquiry to a person. Start cautiously and review its responses.
7. With setup support, test the website enquiry integration using clearly fictional names and an address ending in `example.invalid`. Do not connect real customer traffic until the agreed checks pass.
8. Practise finding an enquiry, reviewing its details, assigning a next action and handling a booking request. Check that saved information remains after refresh.

Do not activate the pilot until access, expiry, support, privacy terms and the agreed retention/deletion procedure are confirmed.

## During the 14 days

Review new enquiries and suggested actions regularly. Check AI answers, quotations and marketing drafts before relying on them or sharing them. Never treat a status such as “Being reviewed” as proof that a customer has received a reply.

Email `support@getbusiness-ai.com` for help. Describe the screen, approximate time, what you expected and what happened. Include a request reference where available. Do not email passwords, authenticator codes, recovery links, unredacted customer records or sensitive screenshots. Support hours and response expectations must be agreed before activation; no round-the-clock response is promised.

If you see another business's information, stop using that screen and contact support immediately. Do not copy, download or forward it. If the AI gives unsafe or invented information, stop the affected customer workflow and report the wording using the approved private support process. Colzo support will assess whether to suspend the affected access or integration through the authorised incident process.

Suggested feedback conversations are on days 3, 7 and 12. These are proposed check-ins, not scheduled messages. Tell us what helped, what confused you and whether a request was missed or wrongly handled. Feedback can use fictional examples.

For account recovery, use the verified Colzo recovery route and contact support if it fails. Do not create another free account to restart access. Do not remove MFA as a workaround.

## When the pilot ends

Access ends at the agreed expiry time. There is no automatic subscription or charge. Support should explain the approved export/return and deletion process before expiry. The retention period and handling of backup copies must be agreed and documented before real customer data is accepted; this draft does not invent a deletion deadline.

We will review your feedback and ask whether you want information about a future paid service. You are free to decline. Any later subscription needs a separate decision and clearly explained terms.
