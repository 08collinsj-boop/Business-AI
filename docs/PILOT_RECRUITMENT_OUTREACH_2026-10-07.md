## Recruitment tracking

Track each prospect through:

1. Not contacted
2. Message sent
3. Replied
4. Interested
5. Pilot application received
6. Onboarded
7. Actually used the product
8. Completed 14 days
9. Would pay: yes / maybe / no
10. Price/value comment
11. Biggest blocker or requested change

Do not count an account signup as a successful Pilot. A useful Pilot is a business that actually uses the product and gives end-of-Pilot feedback.

## End-of-Pilot commercial validation

Ask these in this order so the price question does not influence the earlier feedback.

1. What did Business AI actually help you with during the Pilot?
2. What did you stop using or find unnecessary?
3. What nearly made you stop using it?
4. If the Pilot ended today, would you want to keep using Business AI? Why?
5. If you had to pay for it from tomorrow, would you pay?
6. Without seeing our planned prices, what monthly price would feel fair for the value you got?
7. Which feature would need to improve before you would pay?
8. Would you be comfortable introducing it to another local business?

Record the answer exactly rather than trying to persuade them.

## Success threshold before spending more

The purpose of this Pilot is commercial validation, not vanity signup numbers.

A useful first checkpoint is:
- 5 active businesses complete a meaningful Pilot;
- at least 3 say they would genuinely pay;
- at least 2 can explain a specific time-saving, lead-capture or Marketing benefit;
- there is no repeated critical blocker that makes day-to-day use impractical.

Only after that checkpoint should the deferred commercial-launch costs/settings be reconsidered.

## Outreach rules

- Personalise the first sentence whenever possible.
- Prefer public business-page DMs or a business's published contact route.
- Do not spam the same business across several channels at once.
- One follow-up is enough if they do not reply.
- Respect any request not to be contacted.
- Do not claim existing customers, savings, conversion improvements or other results we have not measured.
- Do not promise AI phone answering - the current AI Receptionist is for website enquiries.
- Keep the Pilot offer accurate: 14 days free, no card/contract during the Pilot, setup help, honest feedback expected.
