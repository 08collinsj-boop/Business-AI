# Privacy request and incident runbook

Status: Pilot operating procedure. Responsibility is assigned to the Business AI operator; the monitored public contact route must be configured before paid public launch.

## Privacy requests

1. Record the request date, requester, business/tenant and request type.
2. Verify identity proportionately before disclosing or changing personal data.
3. Determine whether Business AI is acting as controller or processor for the data.
4. If Business AI is processor, route the request to the relevant business controller and provide technical assistance.
5. Use the tenant-scoped export/anonymisation tools only after authorisation.
6. Record the action, decision, date and any data that could not lawfully be deleted.
7. Escalate unusual, disputed or high-risk requests for professional advice.

Operational owner: **Business AI operator** (or a formally designated replacement recorded in the operating log).
Public contact route: the email configured in `LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL`.

## Personal-data breach response

1. Contain the incident and preserve necessary evidence.
2. Record when Business AI became aware, systems/tenants affected, data categories, approximate numbers and likely consequences.
3. Determine whether Business AI is controller or processor for the affected data.
4. If processor, notify the relevant controller without undue delay under the DPA.
5. If Business AI is controller, assess whether notification to the ICO is required. Where notification is required, make it without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness.
6. If the breach is likely to create a high risk to affected individuals, assess whether those individuals must also be informed without undue delay.
7. Record all breaches, including those not reported to the ICO, and the reason for the notification decision.
8. Remediate the cause and record follow-up actions.

Incident owner: **Business AI operator** (or a formally designated replacement recorded in the operating log).

This runbook is an operating checklist, not legal advice.
