# Colzo: your free 14-day pilot

Draft guide for approved businesses. Joining is not open yet. We will confirm when the technical, privacy and account checks are complete.

## Before you start

Colzo helps organise website enquiries, routine questions and the next steps for your business. It does not answer telephone calls. AI Marketing, customer-visible business replies, push notifications and paid subscriptions are outside this scoped pilot.

The pilot is free, requires no payment card and does not turn into a paid subscription. It lasts exactly 336 hours from the agreed activation time. We will give you the start and end date and time in writing. It supports two authorised people in total, including the owner. Each person uses their own account.

Start with example information. Do not send customer records, passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information to support.

## Getting set up

1. Agree the business, owner and second user with us. We verify ownership and approve the exact accounts before creating access.
2. Follow the approved secure account setup. Verify your email and set up the authenticator on the correct Colzo account. Keep recovery information privately; never send codes to support.
3. Check your business name, service area, services, opening hours and contact route.
4. Add a few accurate answers to common questions in Business Knowledge. Review extracted facts before approving them. Correct anything uncertain.
5. Agree how enquiries should reach your team and choose the receptionist handling style. Start with human oversight.
6. Test using made-up names and contact information. Ask about services and hours, request a quote, request a booking and say “Do not book anything”. Check the results with us.
7. Add the website enquiry link or widget only after we confirm activation. Keep your existing customer contact route available.

## Using Colzo

Check new enquiries and give each one a clear next step. A booking request is not a confirmed appointment. An internal progress status is not a message sent to the customer. Contact customers through your agreed existing channel during this pilot.

Use only the features enabled in your approved workspace. Review AI answers, quotes and actions before relying on them. If a save fails, keep your draft and check the result before submitting again. Refresh after a successful save to confirm the record is there.

## Getting help

Email support@getbusiness-ai.com. Include the screen name, approximate time, what you expected and what happened. Use an example or a redacted description; do not email customer records, passwords, codes or authenticator setup keys.

If you suspect another business's information is visible, stop using that screen and contact support immediately without forwarding the information. If an AI answer is wrong, stop relying on it, correct the approved information and ask support to check before resuming. Keep handling urgent enquiries through your normal human contact route. Colzo is not an emergency service.

## Feedback

After your first few sessions and near the end, tell us: what helped, what slowed you down, anything inaccurate and whether you would choose to use it again. Honest criticism is useful; positive feedback is not a condition of taking part.

## At the end

Protected pilot access ends at the stated time, even if you still have a browser tab open. Expiry is an access restriction, not a promise that records are immediately deleted. Before activation we will agree how you can request data return or deletion and the applicable retention arrangements. Account recovery or signing in again does not restart the pilot.

There are no automatic charges. Any future paid offer is a separate decision. You may decline it.
