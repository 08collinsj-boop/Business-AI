## Proposed pilot terms

The approved workspace is free for exactly 336 elapsed hours, beginning at the
audited activation timestamp and ending at the recorded expiry timestamp. Record
both in UTC and Europe/London; daylight-saving changes do not alter elapsed duration.
No payment card, automatic charge or automatic subscription conversion applies.
Any later paid offer needs a separate agreement. Password reset/sign-in does not
restart access. There are at most two authorised users including the owner and
pending seat reservations; accounts/authenticators must not be shared.

Permitted scope: approved website/customer enquiries, saved leads and owner actions,
quote/booking requests, approved manual business facts, and customer tracking only
where enabled and accepted. Begin with fictional examples. AI phone answering,
live WhatsApp, Marketing generation/automation, billing/subscriptions, knowledge
file extraction, profile-image upload, Connect, Learn My Style, push and unsupported
customer-visible replies are outside this pilot and remain disabled.

The business checks approved facts and reviews outputs/actions. A booking request
is not a confirmed appointment; an internal progress update is not a sent reply.
Maintain the usual human contact route and handle urgent matters directly. Colzo
assists questions, triage and handover; it does not undertake continuous group
monitoring or replace an accountable person. No outcome, saving or continuous
availability guarantee is proposed.

The participant may request withdrawal through the verified support route. The
operator may suspend access to protect accounts/data, investigate faults or enforce
the agreed scope. Preserve audit evidence and explain next steps through approved
communications. Suspension does not create a right to extend expiry automatically.

Support route: support@getbusiness-ai.com, subject to delivery verification. No
response-time promise is made. Send a redacted description, screen and approximate
time; never passwords, codes, authenticator seeds or unnecessary customer records.

Before activation, the operator and business must approve a concrete data return
format, request/identity-verification procedure, post-pilot retention/deletion period,
backup lifecycle, necessary audit/legal retention and responsible party. These are
unresolved decisions, not an implemented 30-day promise. Expiry restricts access;
it does not automatically delete data. No deletion or data-return commitment may be
made until the tenant-scoped process is tested and documented.
