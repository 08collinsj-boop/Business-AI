# Colzo pilot operations and privacy gates

Prepared 25 October 2026. Operational proposal, not activation approval or legal certification.

## Approval record for each business

Record business eligibility (genuine local service business with website enquiries), verified ownership/authority, consented named owner and optional second user, approved feature scope, exact release SHA, environment/project, legal-document versions, support contact and an explicit activation approval. Keep account details private. Do not record passwords or authenticator secrets.

Gate activation on genuine recovery acceptance, reviewed reconciliation, operator workflow acceptance, matching approved Preview, two-account/MFA/recovery acceptance, physical iPhone testing and completed operator/provider/retention documentation. A passing synthetic suite does not waive a gate.

The private operator uses the staged atomic creation/grant flow, an idempotency key and verified owner identity. No public self-grant route; no manual partial business/grant creation. Verify grant, business, membership, route, settings and audit commit together. Invite only the approved second user through the reviewed workflow. Seats include the owner and pending reservations. Preserve audit history on revocation and fail closed on persistence errors.

## Setup and training

Prepare services, hours, service area, contact details and a small approved FAQ. Review facts before activation. Start with an example-data walkthrough. Confirm the difference between a request and a confirmed booking and between progress and a customer reply. Verify website integration and retain the business's existing contact channel. Exclude Marketing and deferred replies from this scoped offer.

## Support and issue process

Use support@getbusiness-ai.com; no guaranteed response time is promised. Maintain one private issue record with a reference, time, release/environment, severity, reproducible example, owner, next action and resolution evidence. Redact personal data. Check support and business-owned enquiries during the agreed pilot working routine; do not promise continuous monitoring.

- Suspected cross-tenant access, account takeover or data exposure: suspend the affected workflow through approved controls, preserve restricted evidence, notify the operator and assess exposure; do not delete audit records or ask users to forward exposed records.
- Incorrect AI output or unwanted booking: stop the affected automation, maintain human contact, reproduce with examples and fix/test before resuming.
- Lost login/MFA: verify the correct identity/project and follow the reviewed recovery flow. No security bypass, shared password or trial extension through a replacement workspace.
- Data rights or complaints: log receipt privately, verify proportionately, identify controller/processor responsibilities and route to the authorised person. Obtain professional advice for legal duties and deadlines; do not improvise a refusal or disclose another person's records.

Collect feedback after first use, around day seven and before expiry through manual, approved communications. Record useful outcomes such as a real demo request, an approved pilot or a resolved workflow problem. Do not infer customer benefit from view counts.

## Expiry and closure

State activation and expiry timestamps explicitly: 336 elapsed hours, UTC record plus Europe/London display. Prepare a manual reminder before expiry and a closure notice; do not enable a scheduler or send these yet. Verify access denial at the boundary and that another tenant remains active. Signing in/resetting a password does not renew access.

Before onboarding, approve the actual data return/deletion window, treatment of backups, necessary audit/legal retention and responsible operator. Current privacy text mentions 365/730-day review settings, not automatic deletion. A possible 30-day post-pilot return window is only a proposal and must not be represented as implemented. Test deletion/return in isolation and record the decision before promises are made. Never silently delete shared records. Offer future subscription interest separately; no automatic billing or conversion.
