*Baseline: 29 October 2026. Existing draft PR #65 only. Execution-oriented acceptance checklist, not authorisation to deploy, change Auth, alter privileges, delete data or activate real businesses.*

## The outcome we are building

Run **one genuine, informed, fully supported 14-day business pilot** with no extra spend, separate from the existing fictional-data demonstration. The owner must be able to sign in with protected access, onboard, receive and manage enquiries, bookings, customers and follow-ups, use the restricted AI Receptionist and knowledge tools, inspect marketing drafts, export data, and reach the operator. All records must remain tenant-isolated and recoverable, with a documented end-of-pilot data lifecycle.

**Definition of PILOT GO:** G1–G7 below independently PASS, real operator/legal/support and retention decisions signed off, and an explicit *separate* authorisation to activate the selected real-business workspace. A green synthetic test count, Preview availability or original owner's MFA is not by itself pilot acceptance.
