## Workflow: interest without arranging a call

1. A business messages the Colzo Page or explicitly permits an individual message. Log a minimal record in the existing tracker: business name (public), contact channel, contact permission, stage, last contact date, consent for one optional follow-up and a brief need. No passwords, customer enquiries, datasets or sensitive business records.
2. Reply with the **approved and independently verified** walkthrough link or private example-data screenshots/video, plus the brief questions below. This is a demo, not an invitation or sign-up.
3. Ask the business to reply at its convenience. Never ask to connect its website, provide real customer information or create an account while release gates remain NO-GO.
4. Mark `Interested in demo`, `Demo watched/feedback`, `Pilot waitlist — consented`, `Not a fit` or `Do not contact` accurately. A video view, reaction, share or unanswered message is **not** pilot consent or a qualified lead.
5. Once G1–G7 are independently PASS and the exact business/owner and activation are separately authorised, send approved legal/pilot terms and an actual onboarding invitation. No earlier message should imply guaranteed access or a fixed start date.

## Demonstration narrative (approx. 45–75 seconds, no slideshow requirement)

- **Problem:** An example customer enquiry arrives while the owner is busy.
- **Capture:** Show a fictional enquiry saved once, with a clearly labelled status and customer context.
- **Next step:** Show a follow-up/action and a request-versus-confirmed booking distinction. Never show or imply an overlapping confirmed booking is allowed.
- **Knowledge:** A fictional business fact sits in review until the owner approves it. Explain that AI should not invent prices or commitments.
- **Marketing:** Show a **manual draft** with a human approval step; say that automated posting is not active in the current pilot scope.
- **Close:** Invite an async reply about the business's hardest enquiry-management problem—not an account signup or a sales call.

Record against the approved runtime with fictional records only. Use the existing Colzo Graphite/Volt styling and review the actual final export for readable UI, transitions, sound and accuracy before sharing.
