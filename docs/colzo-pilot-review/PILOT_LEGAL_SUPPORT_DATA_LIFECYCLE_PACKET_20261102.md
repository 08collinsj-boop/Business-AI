**Offer and start.** A limited pilot of 14 consecutive days (336 hours), starting at the audited activation timestamp **[UTC]**, ending at **[UTC]** (also display both in Europe/London for clarity). **Included use.** Approved enquiries, saved leads, follow-ups, requested/confirmed bookings within the accepted safeguards, reviewed/approved Business Knowledge, and manual Marketing drafts. An enquiry response is not an appointment confirmation. Owners remain responsible for checking facts and managing customer communications and safety-sensitive requests. Disabled or excluded: phone calls, automatic social publishing, AI Marketing generation, payments, and integrations not individually tested/approved.

**Data roles and input limits.** The participating business decides the purpose and legal basis for customer processing and normally acts as Controller; the Colzo operator acts as Processor for those customer records under the DPA. Colzo acts as Controller for its own account/security, support and legal records as described in the Privacy Notice. Do not intentionally supply special-category data, criminal-offence data, payment cards, credentials or unnecessary private records during this restricted pilot. Start with fictional records, then only customer data lawfully authorised after the pilot's security, transparency and exit gates are passed. The business provides suitable privacy information to its customers.

**Support and incident route.** Support/privacy/security requests: the **published and operator-verified** contact route, expected `support@getbusiness-ai.com` if the live legal identity matches and inbox monitoring is confirmed. Provide a brief redacted issue description, approximate time, and business identifier; never send passwords, MFA codes, customer lists or bearer tokens. There is **no guaranteed 24/7 support or fixed service response time** unless separately accepted. Urgent/emergency customer issues should be handled through the business's ordinary human contact route. Colzo may restrict access for reasonable security reasons, with appropriate notice where lawful.

**Service and recovery limitations (express disclosure and opt-in acknowledgment).** The pilot is prerelease software with features that may fail or be unavailable; do not use it as the sole copy of critical business records. At the date of this draft, complete source database/Auth/Storage restore has **not been verified**. A total data-loss or restoration failure cannot currently be ruled out. No recovery-point/time, uptime or error-free operation is promised. This disclosure is **not** a waiver of mandatory data-protection/security responsibilities and cannot replace appropriate safeguards. Business owner must explicitly acknowledge before any real customer-data processing.

**Exit, return, and deletion — NOT YET AN ENFORCEABLE TIME PROMISE.** Expiry terminates workspace access; it does **not** itself erase records. Before activating any business with real data, the parties must fill in and sign the following **operationally feasible**, rehearsed annex: **(i)** requested return format(s), scope and exclusions **[TBD]**; **(ii)** authenticated owner request route and deadline **[TBD]**; **(iii)** handling/secure delivery lead and deadline **[TBD]**; **(iv)** retention purpose(s), exact deletion trigger and deadline **[TBD]**; **(v)** separately controlled Auth/Storage/backup deletion handling and lawful audit exceptions **[TBD]**; **(vi)** proof of tenant-scoped export/deletion rehearsal **[evidence link]**. Article 28 return/delete rights continue to apply independently of any convenience window. Do **not** promise a "30-day deletion" while this capability remains blocked. If no workable annex is approved, limit the session to fictional, non-personal demonstration data instead of activating real-data processing.

**Ending and consent.** Either party may request discontinuation using the verified contact route; the operator may suspend for access/security issues. Record business authority, the exact documents viewed and accepted, the data-risk acknowledgment and the separate owner approval for that named activation. No acceptance is inferred from marketing engagement, Page messages, ordinary onboarding or another account's DPA consent.

## C. Operator playbook — concrete tasks and accountable handling

### 1. Verify the real contact route before using the addendum
- Use the approved protected release's /api/legal-public to confirm all seven identity fields and legal page rendering without leaking private details into CI/PR. Compare identity to the already-authorised Business AI reuse record.
- Confirm that the **support inbox currently receives externally sent mail** and who actually monitors it; perform one explicitly approved non-sensitive delivery test if necessary. Record test time, route, receiving mailbox and acknowledgment without publicising private headers or credentials.
- Identify the **responsible named operator** and a private backup/escalation route for suspected data loss or abuse; keep operator contact details in approved private storage. Verify the published telephone/contact actually reaches the operator; do not fabricate working hours or response promises.
- Acknowledge service contacts and maintain a minimally scoped ticket log: received time, tenant, type, risk/urgency, assigned owner, next step, resolution and notification date. Avoid copying payloads into public GitHub. No auto-ticketing claim.

### 2. Handling privacy rights and complaints
- Distinguish **Colzo's controller data** (account/support) from **business-customer data Colzo processes for the tenant**. Log a request on receipt, avoid unnecessary copying, verify the requestor by an established authenticated channel, forward business-customer rights requests promptly to the tenant controller, and assist under the DPA.
- UK GDPR rights requests normally require action without undue delay and within **one calendar month**, subject to lawful exceptions/extension and identity/clarification rules. Do not promise all requests can be resolved automatically. Escalate immediately if deadlines may be missed.
- Record **data-protection complaints** separately. Current ICO guidance requires a usable complaints route, **acknowledgment within 30 days**, appropriate enquiries, updates, and outcome without undue delay. Acknowledging a complaint is not the same as resolving an access request.
- If a data breach is suspected, immediately record and contain it, notify the tenant controller **without undue delay** as processor, assess risks, preserve an incident record and take advice. Where Colzo is controller, report notifiable UK GDPR breaches to the ICO without undue delay, where feasible **within 72 hours**, and notify affected people where high risk. Do not promise a longer internal handling window that consumes the legal notification deadline.

### 3. Before first real-data activation: return/deletion test and policy signoff
- Agree retention by **data category and reason** (tenant enquiries/customers/bookings/actions/knowledge/marketing, user account/Auth, security/audit, legal consents, support and any provider backups). Record who is controller and the trigger/clock for each category. The existing 365-/730-day UI review settings are **not** disposal schedules.
- Prepare tenant-bound export manifest: CSV/JSON for suitable record sets, attachments and Storage object bytes where in scope, linked data completeness, counts/checksums, field minimisation, third-party or global exclusions, shared-user/Auth dependency treatment. Confirm all returned rows belong to the correct approved business; authenticate destination recipient privately.
- **Rehearse** full-tenant return and safe deletion on synthetic isolated fixtures. No automated scoped-pilot DELETE is accepted. Plan stage-by-stage erasure across DB, Storage, Auth and providers with rollback/failure handling, tenant boundary verification, lawful retention holds, and a non-sensitive audit record. Do not perform destructive reads/writes on shared Development or any real tenant without a separate scoped approval.
- Decide and document the actual customer-facing request/return/deletion periods **after** the test proves they can be achieved. For deliberation only, operator may evaluate a **14-calendar-day request window** and **30-calendar-day target after approved return** for primary copies; **these numbers are not current promises**, must not override lawful data rights or DPA, and need proof of actual capability and backup lifecycle.

If a stage is blocked, disclose it and escalate; never claim erased/returned where the action was only requested.
