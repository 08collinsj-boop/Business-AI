## Outreach compliance
Follow the ICO's [B2B direct-marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/). Cold marketing email to sole traders and some partnerships ordinarily requires consent/valid soft opt-in. Corporate subscribers may not require PECR consent, but messages must identify Colzo, have a valid opt-out, respect objections, and comply with applicable UK GDPR when personal data is used. Do not infer legal status merely from email domain. Do not use customer quote-request forms to spam service providers.
