# Colzo — seven-day organic pilot-interest campaign
**Prepared:** 10 November 2026 (UK)  
**Scope:** Hartlepool / Teesside local small businesses; £0 paid advertising  
**Goal:** Three to five suitable businesses interested in a future, fictional-data-only 14-day pilot. Interest is not activation.

## Current marketing promise and guardrails
- Headline: **Less admin. More business.**
- Explain the actual scoped product: website enquiries, quote/booking **requests**, owner review, follow-up actions and approved business FAQ responses.
- DO NOT promise telephone answering, fully automated booking confirmation, AI Marketing inclusion, real customer-data onboarding, or availability now.
- Pilot is undergoing final acceptance; signup and real-business onboarding are disabled until separately approved. No card, no contract, no automatic paid conversion.
- Example visuals and recorded workflows must be clearly labelled **Illustrative demo — fictional data** unless captured from a genuinely verified approved release.
- No public posts include the founder's name.

## Facebook posting safety
1. Check existing Page feed and scheduled queue before scheduling to prevent duplicates. The seven dates are **creative intentions**, not claims that anything has been scheduled.
2. Post only to a group whose current rules permit it. The Hartlepool Chat group previously rejected one promotion and has another moderated post pending; **do not resubmit there**.
3. Keep promotion in suitable Page/advertising-permitted groups; discussion-only questions for groups where appropriate. Do not use personal names or make unsolicited cold messages.
4. Ask explicit permission before sending direct demos, follow-ups or invites to prior prospects.
5. Do not use unverified image/video as real UI evidence; label concept visuals.

## Measure conversion, not applause
Track per-post: post URL, date, placement, reach, shares, comments, message requests, named-business fit (without unnecessary personal data), demos requested, demos attended, qualified pilot opt-ins. Engagement is not consent or pilot activation.
Targets for first week (not actuals): 10 meaningful conversations, 5 demo requests, 3 qualified interested businesses.
